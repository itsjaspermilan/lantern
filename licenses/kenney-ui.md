# Kenney icons

The button prompts in the home screen's hints (A, B, X, Y, L, R, D-pad) are from Kenney's **Input Prompts**
(Nintendo Switch set, 128 px "Double" sprites), and the arrows and grid icon are from Kenney's **Game Icons**
(white, 2x). Both are Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0); credit is welcome, not required. Source: the "Kenney Game
Assets All-in-1 3.8.0" bundle, https://kenney.nl/assets. The packs' own licence files sit next to this one.
Files: `app/src/main/assets/ui/*.png`.

`btn_square.png` and `btn_triangle.png` (the Retroid's Select and Start) are not from Kenney: they were drawn for Lantern
in the same style (a white disc with the shape cut out) and are MIT like the rest of the project.
